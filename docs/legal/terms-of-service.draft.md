## Account responsibility

Users must provide accurate account information, protect their credentials and
promptly report suspected unauthorized access. Passwords, recovery tokens and
provider credentials must not be shared with support or other users.

## Organizations and roles

The organization owner is responsible for inviting members and assigning roles.
Owners and administrators must grant only the scopes necessary for a member's
work. Users must not attempt to bypass tenant boundaries, RBAC checks, recent
authentication requirements or platform ownership checks.

## Platform resources

Users may connect only Discord servers, Twitch channels and other platform
resources that they are authorized to administer. Muxivo Console may refuse,
degrade, reauthorize or disconnect a connection when ownership, scope,
availability or security checks fail.

Console is a control plane. Availability, API behavior and policy changes of
Discord, Twitch and other third-party platforms remain outside Muxivo's direct
control and are subject to their own terms and rules.

## Acceptable use

Users must not use Console to violate law, third-party platform rules,
community rules, privacy rights or security boundaries. Prohibited activity
includes credential abuse, token extraction, unauthorized resource access,
malware, spam, service disruption and attempts to evade rate limits or audit
controls.

## Suspension and revocation

Muxivo may suspend an account, organization action or platform connection when
there is abuse, an active security risk, a loss of authorization or a serious
availability concern. Where appropriate, revocation and suspension actions are
recorded in the security audit trail and communicated through the approved
support channel.
